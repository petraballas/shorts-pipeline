Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Where the app runs

Shorts Pipeline runs locally on the user's PC. It is a desktop program, not a hosted cloud service that stores your videos or account data on my servers.

What is stored, and where

If you connect TikTok or YouTube, the app stores OAuth tokens and related app credentials only locally on your computer. Those files stay on your PC and are used to sign in and to upload on your behalf.

No collection or sharing of personal data

The app does not collect personal data for me, and it does not share personal data with third parties for advertising, analytics, or resale. I do not operate a backend that receives your account information.

TikTok and YouTube APIs

The app uses the TikTok and YouTube APIs only to upload the user's own videos to the user's own accounts, and to carry out the related sign-in and upload steps those APIs require. Use of those APIs is also subject to TikTok's and YouTube's own terms and privacy policies.

Data deletion

You can revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok account settings (and, for YouTube, in your Google account's third-party access settings). You can also delete the local token and credential files on your computer, or uninstall the app. After you revoke access and delete the local files, the app can no longer use those accounts until you sign in again.

Contact
