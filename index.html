Shorts Pipeline

A personal desktop tool that uploads my own short videos to my TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Shorts Pipeline runs on my computer. It helps me prepare short clips from streams I work with and send those finished videos to the TikTok and YouTube accounts I control.

It is not a public web service and it is not offered as a commercial platform. These pages exist so TikTok and YouTube can review the app: what it is, how it is used, and how data is handled.
